Healthy Scroll — Delete Your Data

Healthy Scroll, by Adolfo Fernández Gómez de Enterría · Last updated: October 19, 2026

Healthy Scroll doesn't ask for an email or password: your account is anonymous and lives on your phone. You can delete it and all of its data yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Healthy Scroll.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.

If you can't use the app, email adolfofge.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Because accounts are anonymous, we may not be able to tell which account is yours from an email alone; deleting from the app is the reliable way.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account ID
  • Your workout and quiz activity: minutes earned and balance, repetition counts, streak, last activity date
  • Your unlock history (when you unlocked a blocked app, and for how many minutes)
  • Your subscription status and customer record at our payments provider, RevenueCat

What is kept

  • A one-way hash of your device identifier, unlinked from any account. It can't be turned back into the original identifier and contains no personal data. We keep it so the free trial week can't be restarted by reinstalling the app.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play or Apple, under their own privacy policies. If you have an active subscription, "Restore purchases" in the app links it to a new account.

More details are in our privacy policy.
