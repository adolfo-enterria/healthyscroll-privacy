Healthy Scroll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Healthy Scroll ("the app", "we", "us") blocks distracting apps until you earn screen time back through exercise or quizzes. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is:
Adolfo Fernández Gómez de Enterría
Madrid, Spain
Contact for privacy requests: adolfofge.dev@gmail.com

2. No account, no email required

Healthy Scroll does not ask for your name, email address, or a password. On first launch, the app creates an anonymous account identified only by a random ID. We never link this ID to your real identity.

3. What we collect, and why

DataWhy
Anonymous account ID Identifies your data on our servers without identifying you personally.
Workout and quiz activity (minutes earned, repetition counts, streaks, last activity date) Powers the core "earn your screen time" mechanic and your progress stats.
When you unlocked a blocked app, and for how many minutes Lets us deduct the minutes you spend from your balance. We do not record which app you unlocked.
A one-way cryptographic hash of a device identifier (Android ID, or an install ID stored in the iOS Keychain) Prevents someone from getting unlimited free trial weeks by reinstalling the app. We store only the hash, which cannot be reversed back into the original identifier.
Subscription status (active/inactive, plan type, renewal date) Unlocks Pro features correctly and lets you restore your purchase on a new device.
Purchase and billing data Handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play and our payments provider, RevenueCat — we never see or store your card details.

4. The camera never leaves your phone

Healthy Scroll counts push-ups, sit-ups, squats and jumping jacks using your phone's front camera. This processing happens entirely on your device, in real time. We do not record, store, upload, or transmit any camera image or video — not to our servers, not to any third party. Nobody but you ever sees the camera feed.

5. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers (data processors) to run the app:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles anonymous sign-in and sync.
  • RevenueCat — validates and manages subscriptions across the App Store and Google Play.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process all payments directly; we never receive your payment details.

We do not use any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we never sell your data to anyone.

6. International data transfers

Some of our service providers (Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where this happens, it relies on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as you use the app. If you don't open the app for an extended period, inactive accounts and their data may be deleted. You can also delete everything immediately — see below.

8. Deleting your data

Open the app, go to Profile → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account and its data from our servers, including your minutes, streak, repetitions and settings. This does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that separately from the App Store or Google Play. You can also email us at adolfofge.dev@gmail.com to request deletion.

We keep only the one-way hash of your device identifier (see section 3), unlinked from any account, so that the free trial week can't be restarted by deleting and reinstalling. It contains no personal data. Step-by-step instructions are on our data deletion page.

9. Your rights

If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, data protection law gives you the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your data, and to object to how we use it. Because your account is anonymous, we can usually only act on requests made from within the app itself (Profile → Delete my data), since we have no other way to verify a request is really yours. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

10. Children

Healthy Scroll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Security

Your account data is protected with row-level security, so it can only be read or modified by your own anonymous account. Your login session is stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android equivalent) and never leaves your device unencrypted.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email adolfofge.dev@gmail.com.
